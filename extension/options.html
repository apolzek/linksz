Bookmarks extension

The extension keeps a session token for this vault, never the access key. The app lists it under Settings > Devices, where it can be signed out.

Address bar: type bm, a space, then what you are looking for.

Right click: “Save page to Bookmarks” or “Save link to Bookmarks” saves to Temporary.

Popup: Alt+Shift+B. A ✓ on the button means the page is saved already.
